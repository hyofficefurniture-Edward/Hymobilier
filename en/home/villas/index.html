A residential project benefits from an overall view. Room proportions, access, light and daily habits guide furniture choices. Fenmi Casa discusses selections using your plans, references and material palette.

Build a shared direction

Gather style references and identify priority rooms. A palette of wood, fabrics and surfaces can connect living, dining and bedroom spaces without making every room identical.

Check dimensions

Compare furniture footprints with measured plans and access routes. Doors, stairs and lifts can affect handling and assembly.

Define what is included

Separate freestanding furniture, drawing-based items and accessories. Services, samples and delivery conditions are defined through the project brief.
