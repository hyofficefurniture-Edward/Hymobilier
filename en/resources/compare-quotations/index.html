PRACTICAL GUIDE

Comparing furniture quotations on a common basis

Review items, specifications and services before comparing totals.

Quotations are comparable only when their scopes are comparable. A lower total may reflect different materials, quantities or services.

Compare the items

Check dimensions, finishes, quantities and composition. A product name alone does not establish equivalence.

Identify services

Samples, packing, transport, importation and assembly may be included, excluded or priced separately. Check each proposal.

Document open questions

Review substitutions, documents, stage-by-stage timing, payment terms and responsibilities before approval. Estimates must remain distinct from contractual conditions.
