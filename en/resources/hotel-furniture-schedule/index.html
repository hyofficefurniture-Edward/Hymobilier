PRACTICAL GUIDE

Preparing a hotel furniture schedule

A space-by-space method for preparing a hotel or renovation furniture brief.

A useful schedule links each item to a space, quantity and reference. It helps teams review the scope and compare proposals.

Start with the spaces

List room types, then reception, dining, meetings and other included areas. Keep typical-room quantities separate from total quantities.

Describe each item

Include function, desired dimensions, material, finish and quantity. Attach a plan or reference when an item depends on a layout.

Separate the services

Samples, transport, receiving and installation should be identified with their responsibilities. Furniture quotations can cover different scopes.
