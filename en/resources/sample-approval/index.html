PRACTICAL GUIDE

Organising sample approval

Record the information connecting samples, drawings and the furniture order.

Clear approval connects a decision with an item, version and responsible person. This reduces uncertainty across documents.

Identify the reference

Describe the item, material and finish. Record the date and reference of the reviewed sample.

Describe the decision

State whether the sample is approved, requires changes or needs another review. Record specific reservations in the brief.

Keep versions consistent

After a change, verify that drawings and furniture schedules use the same version. Photographs do not replace every physical check.
