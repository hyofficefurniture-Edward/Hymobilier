Office furniture for your workplace project

Prepare workstations, meeting rooms, reception and storage around your floor plan.

Image from the group library. Selection and conditions are confirmed for your project.

Workplace projects begin with people and activities. Workstation counts, meeting spaces, circulation and storage needs provide a framework for selection. Hongye discusses configurations and finishes using your layout.

Start with the layout

Specify team size, work patterns and spaces to furnish. Check access routes, door openings and the space around desks and tables.

Coordinate equipment

Define cable routes, displays, power points and conferencing equipment. Visually similar configurations can meet different technical requirements.

Compare specifications

Review dimensions, supports, finishes, adjustments and maintenance needs line by line. Required documents and tests should be identified for the selected models.
