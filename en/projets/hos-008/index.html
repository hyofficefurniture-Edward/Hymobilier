Holiday Inn Fairview Heights: guestroom furniture project reference

Holiday Inn Fairview Heights in the United States is documented in the group hotel project library. The company source separates guestrooms from public areas and illustrates furniture for sleeping, working, sitting and storing luggage. This reference helps a project team define a coordinated furniture schedule; it is not evidence that another order has the same dimensions, materials or delivery conditions.

What the project documentation shows

The published project page presents guestroom furniture alongside lounge, bar and restaurant seating. Its room section also shows writing-area seating, storage, luggage-related pieces and bathroom furniture. Only the reviewed guestroom photograph is reproduced here; these other spaces are described as source documentation, not represented by an unrelated image.

Read the guestroom image as a layout reference

The guestroom photograph supports a discussion of how the bed, bedside pieces and seating share one room. Compare the position of each item with doors, usable circulation and the intended activities. A photograph cannot provide reliable hidden dimensions, finish specifications or fixing details; use measured plans to confirm those points.

Turn the reference into a procurement brief

Separate the request by room type and furniture item. Ask the design and procurement teams to identify what is fixed, what is movable and which accessories are included. When discussing seating or a writing area, record the intended use and the available clearances rather than copying a catalogue size without checking the room.

Interfaces to confirm before production

Confirm power and lighting locations, cable access, furniture-to-wall junctions and delivery access. Review material samples and the approved finish schedule together so that adjacent pieces can be coordinated. These are recommendations for a new project, not a claim that the original project used a particular installation or approval procedure.

Evidence and next steps

The public company page establishes the project reference and the documented furniture categories. It does not replace product-specific test reports or the technical schedule for a new order. Room counts, performance guarantees and brand compliance claims are deliberately excluded until separately substantiated.
