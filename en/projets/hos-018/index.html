Project: TOA Hotel & Spa Zanzibar — Tanzania

TOA Zanzibar resort reference: plan room schedules, coastal exposure checks, samples and delivery interfaces for a new hotel furniture enquiry.

Visual extracted from this project’s group documentation.

Resort spaces

A Tanzania resort reference with defined evidence limits

The reference identifies TOA Hotel & Spa in Zanzibar, Tanzania. The existing views show the resort setting and several spaces. They are not a complete supplied-furniture schedule and do not establish that the group supplied every visible piece or fitting.

Build a schedule for each room type

For a new resort, separate guestrooms, suites and shared spaces in a quantity matrix. Position the bed, headboard, storage and seating on measured plans, then review door movement and access around each item. Confirm the models requested for the new order separately from the original reference.

Treat coastal exposure as a specification question

Identify indoor, sheltered and exposed locations before comparing materials. Explain the intended humidity, maintenance and storage conditions and request evidence for the chosen material and hardware. The Zanzibar location alone proves neither salt resistance nor outdoor suitability for another order.

Agree samples and the delivery handover

Select a representative room type for sample and junction review. Record revisions before repeating the approved configuration. For an island delivery, define the receiving point, unloading access, storage and assembly responsibility; transport arrangements and services remain subject to the new contract.

Does the resort reference prove outdoor durability?

No. Intended exposure, chosen materials and relevant product evidence must be verified for the selected model. The project photographs do not replace that review.

What helps prepare a comparable resort enquiry?

Measured plans, room types, an item and quantity schedule, destination and receiving conditions. Quote HOS-018 and identify the spaces you want to discuss; the images are not a supply offer.
