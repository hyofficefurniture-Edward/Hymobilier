Procurement discussion for a new order

The following guidance concerns a new project; it is not an additional claim about the historical delivery.

Keep the Yanbu project separate from a new site

Use the Saudi Arabian hotel reference as historical project context. It does not establish that the same selection, quantities or services are available for every new Saudi enquiry.

Specify exposed and sheltered use

For a new order, mark which furniture locations are indoors, sheltered or exposed. A coastal destination alone does not prove corrosion, salt-spray or weather performance; ask for selected-item evidence.

Coordinate samples and documents

Match finish samples and dimensional drawings to each proposed item. Separate furniture manufacture from electrical, building and installation works in the new written scope.

Plan the destination handover

Confirm packaging, unloading, access and acceptance responsibilities with the project parties. A past Saudi hotel reference is not evidence of a local warehouse, permanent branch or unconditional installation service.

Does the photograph prove the complete supply scope?

No. Confirm the selected items and responsibilities against project-specific documents. The new procurement guidance is separate from historical delivery facts.
