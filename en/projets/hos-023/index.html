Identify the Zhanjiang evidence

Keep this project identified as Zhanjiang, China. Do not transfer Guangzhou brand-history photographs or claims into this project; any additional image requires a matching project segment.

Label photo and design identities

Distinguish documented room photographs from design representations. Neither image type alone proves the complete supplied furniture inventory, manufacturing material or delivery quantity.

Prepare the new room package

For a new enquiry, map bedsides, desks, storage and circulation to a measured room drawing. Treat the discussion as proposed procurement coordination rather than an extension of the historical supply record.

Confirm samples and site responsibilities

Approve selected-item drawings and finish samples and record assembly, unloading and acceptance responsibilities. Do not infer unrecorded dimensions, fire performance or an overseas branch from this Chinese hotel reference.
