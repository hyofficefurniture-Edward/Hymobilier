Documented project context

The company project presentation identifies Elaf Hotel in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, and lists lobbies, suites, restaurant, massage and meeting spaces. These are the documented categories of the reference; they do not make every furniture item shown a specification for another hotel.

Separate the rooms from the public areas

Create a schedule by space: suite furniture, reception or lobby seating, dining pieces and meeting-room furniture serve different uses. For a new project, state the number of each room type and the activities in each shared area before asking for matching pieces.

Resolve fixed and loose furniture interfaces

Mark wall junctions, power points, circulation and access on measured plans. Review how wardrobes, beds and movable seating meet the room layout. Samples, dimensions and accessory scope should be approved separately; this is a preparation method, not a claim about an unverified procedure used on the original project.

What a photograph cannot establish

The existing project images are retained with their original reference role. They cannot verify hidden dimensions, a sensor specification, fire performance, a material test or a guaranteed delivery period. Ask for the documents applicable to the selected items and the intended destination.
