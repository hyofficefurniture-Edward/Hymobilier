Project: The Ebrington Hotel Londonderry — United Kingdom

Ebrington Hotel reference: prepare room schedules, technical interfaces, samples and receiving for a new furniture order.

Visual extracted from this project’s group documentation.

Locate the project and its documented scope

The Ebrington Hotel Londonderry is a hotel reference in Northern Ireland, United Kingdom. The document image identifies the setting; it is not a complete supplied-item, material or quantity schedule.

Build a room-type matrix

For a new hotel, position beds, storage, luggage and loose seating on measured plans for each room type. Separate fixed joinery and movable pieces; keep reception, dining and meeting schedules distinct to compare like-for-like quotations.

Assign technical interfaces

Agree who confirms sockets, lighting, access panels, skirting and wall junctions before drawings are approved. Check maintenance access. These are checks for the new brief, not unverified features of the original Ebrington project.

Track samples and receiving

Keep finish references, revisions and sample-room observations in one approval record. Link carton labels to rooms or zones, then define storage and assembly responsibilities. The international reference does not establish a delivery date or local service for another order.

Do hotel quantities equal supplier quantities?

That cannot be established from this page. Prepare an item and quantity schedule for the new order and confirm it against selected models and drawings.

How can two furniture quotations be compared?

Use the same items, dimensions, finishes, accessories and quantities, then separate packing, transport and assembly. Quote HOS-019 as a discussion reference.
