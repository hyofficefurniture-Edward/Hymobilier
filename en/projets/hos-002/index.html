Keep the documented spaces separate

The article discusses guestrooms, reception, dining and meeting areas. It does not establish an exhaustive contractual inventory, delivered quantities or final material specifications. Operator room counts and amenities must not be converted into furniture supply quantities.

Use the images as spatial references

The selected lobby and conference images show reception furniture, storage, a table and chairs. They explain the spaces without establishing that every visible item was supplied. They do not confirm a complete contract inventory.

Prepare a new schedule for the destination

For a new brief, separate room types from shared-area schedules and mark dimensions, door swings and technical interfaces. Confirm selected samples, packing, receiving access and assembly responsibilities in the new quotation. These are procurement recommendations, not claims about the original contract.

Does the reference prove local stock or installation services?

No. The documented property is in Abuja, Nigeria. Destination-specific stock, transport and installation arrangements must be agreed for the new order.

Does this reference verify a manufacturer model?

No. Select and verify models and specifications separately for a new order.
