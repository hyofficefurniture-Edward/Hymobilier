Procurement discussion for a new order

The following guidance concerns a new project; it is not an additional claim about the historical delivery.

Plan usable clearance

For a new room package, mark doors, curtains, circulation and access around the proposed furniture. Check the selected-item drawing against measured site conditions rather than estimating dimensions from a photograph.

Coordinate furniture heights

Compare bedside, bed and desk heights within the new room schedule. Ask for the selected configuration and any interface tolerances in writing; the historical reference is not a dimensioned production drawing.

Assign finish references

Link each ordered item to a finish sample and drawing revision. Avoid treating a similar colour in two photographs as proof that the same material or manufacturer model was used.

Define acceptance by item

Record quantities, ordered options and assembly responsibilities for the new order. Do not extend this Chinese reference into a claim of delivery, stock or a local team in a different market.

Does the photograph prove the complete supply scope?

No. Confirm the selected items and responsibilities against project-specific documents. The new procurement guidance is separate from historical delivery facts.
