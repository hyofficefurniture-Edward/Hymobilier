Airport Fliport Hotel, Lanzhou: preparing an international hotel-furniture brief

The group identifies Airport Fliport Hotel in Lanzhou, China, with guestroom and shared-space references. This Chinese hotel is an international group reference, not a completed installation in France or Francophone Africa.

What the source establishes

The group article identifies Airport Fliport Hotel in Lanzhou, China and discusses guestrooms, the lobby, dining and meeting spaces. This establishes the setting, not a complete inventory of supplied furniture.

How to read the guestroom photograph

The reviewed guestroom image can support a discussion of furniture arrangement and circulation. It does not establish dimensions, material grades, performance, contract quantities or the supply of every visible accessory.

Make the consultation usable across project teams

For a new hotel, provide room types, quantities and measured plans. Identify fixed joinery, movable furniture and accessories separately so that a design reference can become a reviewable order schedule.

Confirm the destination requirements for the new order

Discuss selected-item samples and documents, then agree packing, transport, building access and installation responsibilities for your destination. The original reference does not establish delivery times, certificates or a local branch for another project.

Does this photograph identify a furniture model?

No. Select a documented product separately; the project image does not verify a manufacturer SKU.

Is this a local project in the destination market?

No. The verified reference is in Lanzhou, China. A new destination requires its own scope and delivery agreement.
