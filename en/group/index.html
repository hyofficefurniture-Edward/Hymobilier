From the first brief to after-sales support

A complete brief separates room types, fixed joinery, loose furniture and adjacent trades. Confirm drawings, finish samples and an agreed item schedule before production. Site measurement, installation, freight and after-sales responsibilities are agreed for each order; this process is not a promise that every service is included.

Which evidence should be confirmed for the selected furniture?

Request the selected model’s dimensions, materials, finish samples and applicable test or certification documents. A factory area, hotel reference or brand logo does not certify every product or establish a new project’s supply scope.
