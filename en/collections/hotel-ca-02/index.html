Chairs for hotel banquet rooms

Banquet chairs: prepare room layouts, storage, component selection and item-specific evidence for the venue.

Catalogue presentation visuals do not establish an on-site delivery, performance or the exact composition of an order.

Separate the room layouts

The catalogue presentation shows chairs in rows and around tables. Prepare a plan for each banquet use, including seats in use and staff access. Validate circulation and venue requirements; the image does not establish the room’s permitted occupancy.

Plan storage and handling

If stacking is required, request the selected chair reference, instructions and applicable limit. Define storage space and required trolleys without inferring a stack quantity from a photograph.

Approve the chair components

Compare upholstery, frame, glides and finish against an approved sample. Request documents for the venue requirements and selected chair rather than treating a general certification claim as evidence for the entire range.

Separate seating from room equipment

List chairs, tables, trolleys and accessories separately. State room-change frequency, delivery access and storage space to prepare a comparable proposal.

What stacking height is guaranteed?

The visual establishes no stacking height. Confirm the limit and method for the selected chair.

Is a general fire-compliance statement enough?

Request documents matching the upholstery, selected construction and venue requirements.
