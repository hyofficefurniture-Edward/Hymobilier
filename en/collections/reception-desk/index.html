Reception counter selection for office lobbies

Office-lobby reception counters: define staff positions, counter levels, circulation and supplied items from the project plan.

Image from the group library. Selection and conditions are confirmed for your project.

Catalogue visuals; define the composition and accessories in the order schedule. These are not delivery photographs.

Define the counter from the lobby plan

This catalogue reference presents office-lobby counters without establishing one model for every composition. Mark staff positions, staff access and visitor circulation on the plan; an illustrated setting is not a supply schedule.

Separate visitor reception from the working position

Identify where visitors exchange documents and where staff use a monitor, phone and storage. Confirm heights, clearances and applicable accessibility requirements for the project rather than measuring a photograph.

Approve samples and the item schedule

Compare surfaces, furniture body and junctions against approved samples and drawings. List chairs, modules and companion furniture separately in the quotation.

Prepare receiving

Share destination, access routes, opening dimensions and receiving floor. Agree packing, handling and assembly responsibility for the order.

Does the reference establish one counter height?

No. Confirm visitor and working levels and the project’s accessibility requirements on the order drawing.

Is the lobby decoration included?

Only items named in the supply schedule are included. Separate the counter, seating, lighting and building finishes.
