High tables for hospitality spaces

Hospitality high tables: confirm heights, seating, layout, components and the project’s supply scope.

Catalogue presentation visuals do not establish an on-site delivery, performance or the exact composition of an order.

Pair the table with the selected seating

This reference shows high-table and seating compositions for hospitality spaces. Confirm tabletop and seat heights and usable legroom. Room perspective cannot establish these dimensions.

Test the arrangement in the venue

Draw tables, seating and service routes on the bar or dining-area plan. Review supports and floor conditions for the selected pieces. A catalogue setting does not prove stability or a load rating.

Confirm components and cleaning

Specify the top, base and finishes, then request a sample and care instructions. Catalogue words such as wood or metal do not by themselves define the ordered components or their performance.

Clarify accessories and installation

Separate tables, seating, lighting and bar equipment. State access conditions, handling constraints and assembly responsibilities. Confirm these points for the new project.

Does the photograph establish an exact height?

No. Confirm table height, seat height and clearance for the selected configuration.

Is seating automatically included?

Only when the supply schedule explicitly lists the seats, quantities and configuration.
