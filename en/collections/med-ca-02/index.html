Lab Bench with Sink And Drawers for Hospital Lab: prepare your project selection with dimensions, intended use and finish references.

Describe the activity

List the activities and objects to be accommodated before choosing a layout. The public reference describes a sink bench with drawers; it does not establish chemical resistance or a clinical function.

Allocate building connections

Have the project team mark supplies, drainage and maintenance access. Assign responsibility for taps, connections and building works explicitly; a sink in a picture does not define the equipment package.

Approve the selected construction

Request a dimensioned drawing and selected-item information for the worktop, fronts and wet area. Performance and permitted cleaning products need specific documentation.

Coordinate acceptance

Check site dimensions and access before manufacture and agree the handover interfaces between trades. Testing building services belongs to competent project parties rather than catalogue interpretation.
