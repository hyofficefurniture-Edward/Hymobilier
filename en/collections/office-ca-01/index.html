Executive desk MOSCA

MOSCA: review room layout, return, storage and interfaces and samples before confirming the furniture order.

Compare the two MOSCA formats

The manufacturer presents the MOSCA family with MK-D0326 and MK-D0424 and reference formats of 2600 × 2000 × 760 mm and 2400 × 2000 × 760 mm. These are family options: match the selected code to an approved drawing before ordering.

Orient the return within the room

Draw the desk and return alongside doors, storage and the chair in use. Confirm the return side and access to the working position; an image alone does not establish usable clearance.

Approve samples and the item schedule

Compare tabletop finish, base and junctions against approved samples and drawings. List chairs, modules and companion furniture separately in the quotation.

Prepare receiving

Share destination, access routes, opening dimensions and receiving floor. Agree packing, handling and assembly responsibility for the order.

How should the desk return be confirmed?

Approve the return side, chair in use and storage opening zones on a measured plan, then confirm the drawing for the ordered configuration.

Are chairs and equipment included?

Only if they are listed in the agreed supply schedule. Identify chairs, accessories, modules and services separately.
