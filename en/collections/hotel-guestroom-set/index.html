Hotel guestroom furniture composition

Hotel guestroom composition: define room-type schedules, technical interfaces and samples to confirm before ordering.

Image from the group library. Selection and conditions are confirmed for your project.

Catalogue presentation visuals do not establish an on-site delivery, performance or the exact composition of an order.

Build a schedule for each room type

The source presents a bedroom composition with a bed, headboard and companion furniture. Create a room-type matrix, then separate fixed pieces, movable furniture and accessories. A complete room view does not mean every visible element is included in the quotation.

Resolve interfaces before drawing approval

Mark doors, sockets, lighting and technical access points on measured plans. Review the headboard, bedside units, desk and circulation around the bed. Hidden dimensions and connections cannot be established from the visual.

Define samples for the room mock-up

Link each sample to an item, drawing and revision. Confirm surfaces, edges, textiles and accessories for the selected composition. General lifetime or resistance claims do not replace evidence for the chosen product.

Organise the supply by zone

Provide room counts and item quantities, keeping furniture separate from equipment and building finishes. Agree packing, room identification and assembly responsibilities for your own order.

Is every visible room element included?

No. Identify furniture, accessories and equipment separately in the quotation.

Can the photograph alone define fabrication?

No. The order needs measured plans, quantities, materials and approved drawings for the selected pieces.
