Meeting table BUGATTI

BUGATTI: review room layout, seating, technical interfaces and samples before confirming the furniture order.

Select a length within the BUGATTI family

The source groups BD-C0142 and BD-C0136 with 4200 × 1600 × 750 mm and 3600 × 1600 × 750 mm references. These are options in one family, not two new products. Match the chosen code to the confirmed drawing.

Position seats around the base

Review geometric supports against chair positions and circulation. Check legroom, cable routes and component handling for the selected configuration; table length alone does not establish meeting capacity.

Approve samples and the item schedule

Compare tabletop finish, base and junctions against approved samples and drawings. List chairs, modules and companion furniture separately in the quotation.

Prepare receiving

Share destination, access routes, opening dimensions and receiving floor. Agree packing, handling and assembly responsibility for the order.

How many seats should be specified?

The count depends on chairs, supports and available clearance. Approve a layout against the room plans before confirming the chair quantity.

Are chairs and equipment included?

Only if they are listed in the agreed supply schedule. Identify chairs, accessories, modules and services separately.
