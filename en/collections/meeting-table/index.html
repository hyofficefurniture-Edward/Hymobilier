Meeting table WISDOM

WISDOM: review room layout, seating, technical interfaces and samples before confirming the furniture order.

Image from the group library. Selection and conditions are confirmed for your project.

Plan a WISDOM meeting composition

The manufacturer identifies WISDOM as YZ-C0142 and presents a ten-seat configuration. Position the table and proposed chairs on measured plans to check pull-back space, end access and door movement. Final capacity depends on the selected layout.

Specify the central connections

The central tabletop zone helps define connection needs. List devices, cable routes and maintenance access, then confirm modules and power connections on the order drawing.

Approve samples and the item schedule

Compare tabletop finish, base and junctions against approved samples and drawings. List chairs, modules and companion furniture separately in the quotation.

Prepare receiving

Share destination, access routes, opening dimensions and receiving floor. Agree packing, handling and assembly responsibility for the order.

How many seats should be specified?

The count depends on chairs, supports and available clearance. Approve a layout against the room plans before confirming the chair quantity.

Are chairs and equipment included?

Only if they are listed in the agreed supply schedule. Identify chairs, accessories, modules and services separately.
