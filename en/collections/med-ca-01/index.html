Care-room bedside storage: define the contents

Selected-item photographs are withheld pending source verification. This is a procurement reference, not a manufacturer model specification.

Hospital Plastic ABS Bedside Cabinet with Drawers: prepare your project selection with dimensions, intended use and finish references.

Locate the cabinet

Mark the bed side, circulation route and required opening direction on the room plan. Confirm dimensions for the selected item rather than copying a measurement from another cabinet.

Separate storage needs

List personal belongings, consumables and anything requiring controlled access. A visible drawer does not establish lock type, accessory inclusion or suitability for storing medicines.

Request item documents

Ask for the selected-item data sheet, finish sample and manufacturer cleaning instructions. Do not infer disinfectant compatibility, antibacterial performance or medical compliance from a catalogue photograph.

Define acceptance

List cabinet, locking options, any castors and assembly separately. Check delivered quantities, agreed dimensions and ordered options against the written order.
