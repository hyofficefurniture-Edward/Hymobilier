Outdoor dining set: hospitality selection

Hotel outdoor dining: review layout, exposure, components and care requirements for your terrace selection.

Catalogue presentation visuals do not establish an on-site delivery, performance or the exact composition of an order.

Start with the terrace plan

The group source presents outdoor dining sets in several compositions. Draw the table, chairs in use and service routes on your plan. Seating capacity depends on the selected furniture and clearances, not on counting places in a presentation image.

Describe exposure conditions

State whether the terrace is sheltered, when it is used and the expected rain, sunlight and storage conditions. Request evidence for the selected finish and components; this visual reference does not establish weather or salt-air resistance.

Confirm materials and care against samples

Specify the top, base, seating and cushions separately. Confirm selected materials and care instructions; the page’s commercial name does not replace an item-specific order specification.

Prepare delivery and storage

List quantities by zone, included accessories and storage needs. Confirm packaging, receiving access and assembly responsibilities in the new quotation.

How many seats can be specified?

Approve the table, chairs and pull-back space on the plan. An illustrated composition does not guarantee a seat count for every project.

Does the visual prove suitability for a coastal location?

No. Request evidence and care instructions for the selected components and actual site conditions.
